# Simple Vendor Sales Management Guide

## Overview
A clean, beginner-friendly interface for vendors to track their sales and earnings.

## What You See

### Summary Cards (Top Section)
1. **Total Sales Amount**: Total money from all completed orders
2. **My Earnings**: Your share (90% of total sales)
3. **Orders Completed**: Number of successfully delivered orders

### Filter Section
- **Status Filter**: Choose to see "All Orders", "Pending", or "Completed"
- Simple dropdown - just select and it filters automatically

### Sales Table
Shows your sales records with these columns:
- **Order ID**: Unique order number (e.g., #0001)
- **Product Name**: Which product was sold (e.g., Bansuri, Khaijhandi)
- **Quantity**: How many pieces were ordered
- **Total Price**: Amount customer paid
- **Status**: Either "Pending" or "Completed"

## How to Use

### View All Sales
- When you open the page, you see all your orders
- Summary cards show totals for completed orders only
- Table shows both pending and completed orders

### Filter by Status
1. Click the "Status" dropdown
2. Choose "Pending" to see orders waiting to be delivered
3. Choose "Completed" to see finished orders
4. Choose "All Orders" to see everything

### Understanding Status
- **Pending**: Orders that are accepted/processing but not yet delivered
- **Completed**: Orders that have been delivered to customers

## Key Information

### Earnings Calculation
- You earn 90% of each sale
- Platform keeps 10% as commission
- Earnings are calculated only from completed orders

### Order Status Flow
```
Pending → Completed
```

### Important Notes
- Only completed orders count toward your earnings
- Pending orders show potential future earnings
- All amounts are shown in Nepali Rupees (Rs.)
- Orders are sorted by newest first

## Simple Design Features
- **Clean Layout**: Easy to read cards and table
- **Clear Numbers**: Large, bold text for important amounts
- **Color Coding**: Green for completed, yellow for pending
- **Mobile Friendly**: Works perfectly on phones and tablets
- **No Complex Features**: Just the essentials you need

This simple system helps you track your sales without confusion!
