About Us

Sanskriti Bazar is dedicated to promoting and preserving the rich musical heritage of Nepal. We work directly with skilled artisans and craftsmen to bring you authentic, high-quality traditional Nepali musical instruments.


Our mission is to support local artisans while making these beautiful instruments accessible to music lovers around the world. Each instrument in our collection is carefully crafted using traditional methods passed down through generations.


By choosing Sanskriti Bazar, you're not just buying an instrument – you're supporting Nepali culture and helping preserve these ancient musical traditions for future generations.
